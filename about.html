Why Most AI Projects Fail — And How We Build Differently

Over 80% of corporate generative AI initiatives stall in prototype purgatory. Why? Because connecting an API key to a quick demo is easy; building an auditable, secure, low-latency, and failure-tolerant system integrated with legacy backends is difficult.

At PJB Software, we approach artificial intelligence through the lens of seasoned software engineering. We don't sell ungrounded chat interfaces or fragile scripts. We engineer resilient distributed pipelines, deterministic validation safeguards, and automated workflows that run reliably day in and day out.

Our goal is simple: ensure every automation deliverable drives direct, verifiable operational cost reduction or revenue acceleration.
